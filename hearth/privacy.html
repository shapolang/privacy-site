Privacy Policy

Effective November 14, 2026

Hearth does not have an account system or cloud sync. Everything you enter stays in the app's private storage on your device. We cannot see it, and we have no way to recover it for you. Hearth is free and supported by ads served through Google AdMob, which uses a network connection and handles ad-related data as described below.

What Hearth collects

Hearth itself does not collect, sell, rent, or share your household records. The only third-party data collection comes from Google AdMob for advertising, described below.

Where your data lives

Tasks, calendar entries, notes, food inventory, recipes, meals, medicines, health entries, kids' records, supplies, and settings are stored in an app-private file on your device. Android prevents other apps from reading this file. Uninstalling Hearth deletes it.

Backups and exports

You can create a backup from Settings → Data → Export a backup. The app writes a JSON file to the location you choose using Android's system file picker. That file is under your control; if you save it to a cloud drive, that provider's terms apply. Hearth does not upload it anywhere.

You can restore a backup from Settings → Data → Import a backup, and delete all records with Settings → Data → Delete everything.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Hearth is free and supported by ads served by Google AdMob. The Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google's User Messaging Platform may collect and share:

  • Device or other identifiers, including the Android advertising ID
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address
  • App and ad interactions
  • Diagnostics about SDK performance

This data is used to serve, measure, and improve ads and is processed by Google under its own policies (policies.google.com/technologies/ads). Hearth does not receive or store your household records through AdMob. Where required (for example in the EEA or UK), a consent form is shown before ads are requested, and you can change your choices in Settings → Ad privacy settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily check-in, Hearth asks for the Android notification permission and schedules a local alarm. Event reminders are scheduled the same way, 30 minutes before the event. Notification text is generated on your device from your own records. Hearth uses the "run at startup" permission only to restore those local reminders after a reboot.

Health and children's data

Health, medicine, growth, homework, and baby-care entries are personal records that you choose to store. They are not medical advice and are never transmitted to Hearth or shared with AdMob. Hearth is intended for adults managing a household. We do not knowingly collect information from children — the app itself collects no personal information from anyone.

Third parties

Hearth uses Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform for advertising and consent. It contains no analytics SDKs, crash reporters, or social integrations of our own. It is built with open-source Android libraries (AndroidX, Jetpack Compose, Kotlin, kotlinx.serialization) that run on the device.

Your choices

  • Edit or delete any record inside the app at any time.
  • Export a copy of your data.
  • Delete everything from Settings → Data → Delete everything.
  • Manage ad consent in Settings → Ad privacy settings, or reset your advertising ID in Android settings.
  • Uninstall the app to remove all local data.

Changes

If the app's data practices change (for example, if a future version adds optional cloud sync), this policy will be updated before that feature ships and the effective date will change.
