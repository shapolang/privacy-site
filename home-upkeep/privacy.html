Privacy Policy for Home Upkeep

Last updated: November 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes Our policies and procedures regarding privacy when You use the application Home Upkeep. We respect Your privacy and are committed to being transparent about how information is handled when You use the Application.

Interpretation and Definitions

Interpretation. Words with capitalized initials have the meanings defined below. These definitions shall have the same meaning regardless of whether they appear in singular or plural.

Definitions. For the purposes of this Privacy Policy:

  • Application refers to Home Upkeep, the software provided by the Company.
  • Company (referred to as "We", "Us", or "Our") refers to the Home Upkeep application.
  • Device means any device that can run the Application, such as a smartphone or tablet.
  • Personal Data means information that can identify an individual.
  • Service refers to the Application.
  • You means the individual using the Service.

Data Collection and Use

The Application itself does not directly collect, store, or transmit Your household records or other personal information. Tasks, notes, food inventory, meals, medicine, health entries, kids' records, supplies, and settings are stored locally on Your Device.

However, the Application uses third-party advertising services that may collect certain information automatically to provide and improve advertisements.

Information that may be collected by third-party advertising services includes:

  • Advertising ID or other device identifiers
  • IP address
  • Advertising interaction and impression data
  • App usage and diagnostic information
  • Device information, such as device model and operating system

This information may be used for advertising delivery, ad measurement, fraud prevention, and personalized advertising where permitted by applicable laws and Your consent preferences.

Advertising

The Application uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

Google AdMob is a third-party advertising service provided by Google LLC. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies, advertising identifiers, and similar technologies to deliver, measure, and personalize advertisements.

For more information about how Google handles information, please visit:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

You can manage Your advertising and privacy preferences through the consent options presented in the Application, where applicable. In Home Upkeep, this is available under Settings → Ad privacy settings.

Local Data and Permissions

The Application stores Your household records in a private app directory on Your Device. Android prevents other apps from reading this data, and uninstalling the Application removes it.

The Application may request the following permissions:

  • Notifications — used only to deliver the optional daily check-in and event reminders You enable.
  • Run at startup — used only to restore Your local reminders after the Device restarts.
  • Internet and network state — used only to load Google AdMob advertisements and consent information. Your household records are not uploaded.
  • Advertising ID — used by Google AdMob for advertising, subject to Your consent choices.

Backups are created only when You choose Settings → Data → Export a backup. The file is saved to the location You select using Android's system file picker.

The Application uses the following third-party service:

Google AdMob

AdMob may collect and process certain device and advertising-related information to provide advertisements, measure advertising performance, and prevent fraudulent activity.

Data Sharing

The Application does not directly sell or share Your personal information. We do not receive Your household records.

However, information collected by Google AdMob may be processed by Google and its advertising partners in accordance with their own privacy policies and applicable laws.

Data Retention

The Application does not maintain a database of users' personal information on a server. Household records remain on Your Device until You delete them in the Application, clear the app's data, or uninstall the Application.

Any information collected by third-party advertising services is subject to their respective data retention policies.

Children's Privacy

The Application does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. Home Upkeep is intended for adults managing a household.

Third-party advertising services may process certain device and advertising-related information. Advertising displayed to users is subject to applicable child protection and advertising regulations.

Security

We strive to ensure the Application remains safe and secure. Household records are kept in Android's app-private storage. Third-party service providers are responsible for maintaining the security of information they process in accordance with their own policies.

Links to Other Websites

The Application may refer to third-party privacy policies and service information. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of external websites.
